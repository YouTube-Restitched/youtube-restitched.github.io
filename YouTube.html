YouTube Restitched

Step 1 of 8

1. Download the Extension

Visit the official repository at https://github.com/YouTube-Restitched/youtube-restitched.github.io and download the project ZIP archive.

2. Extract the ZIP

Locate the downloaded ZIP file on your computer and extract its contents into a dedicated folder.

3. Open Extensions Page

Open your browser (compatible with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, etc.) and navigate to:

chrome://extensions/

4. Enable Developer Mode

In the top-right corner of the extensions page, toggle the switch to turn on Developer mode.

5. Load Unpacked

Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner, navigate through your folders to select the innermost folder of the extracted project, and click "Select Folder".

6. Configure Settings

Visit youtube.com, click on the extension icon in your browser toolbar to open the control panel, and make sure to turn off the "Icons only under the video (no text)" feature, as it currently does not work properly and hides buttons.

7. Enjoy & Use

You're all set! Enjoy your cleaner, customized classic YouTube experience.
